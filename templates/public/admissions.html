Admissions & Fee Structure

Transparent admissions, merit-friendly environment, and accessible education for every child in Hazipur and surrounding districts.

Simple 4-Step Admission Journey

Step 1

Inquiry & Form

Collect the Application Form from school office (9 AM - 2 PM) or fill the online contact inquiry form.

Step 2

Document Verification

Submit birth certificate, previous school transfer certificate (if applicable), and passport-size photographs.

Step 3

Friendly Interaction

An informal readiness session for child and brief interaction with parents to align on learning aspirations.

Step 4

Enrollment & ID

Payment of admission session fee, receipt issuance, uniform measurements, and parent portal activation.
